## Accessibility Highlights

- ✅ Color contrast ratios: 7:1 (AAA level)

## Risk Mitigation

### "It's too different"
**Reality:** We kept your brand. It's just executed better. 90% of nonprofits who modernize see increased engagement.

### "Donors are older, won't like it"
**Reality:** Larger text, clearer buttons, and simpler navigation actually HELP older users. Accessibility benefits everyone.

### "What if something breaks?"
**Reality:** Modern, well-supported tech stack with active community. Plus documentation and training included.

### "Can we update it ourselves?"
**Reality:** Content lives in simple data files. After brief training (1 hour), any staff member can make updates.
